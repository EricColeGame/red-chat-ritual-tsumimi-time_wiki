## Chat Etiquette: How to Participate Without Getting Overlooked

Etiquette is not about gatekeeping; it is about protecting the thing everyone showed up for. The Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME Mashiro only works if the response reads as one voice rather than a hundred competing ones.

- **Read the pinned message first.** It usually restates the cue in plain language.
- **Match the format you see.** If the room is sending two words, do not send two paragraphs.
- **Keep it short.** Brevity is what makes a coordinated chat feel coordinated.
- **Do not front-run the cue.** Waiting two seconds is not hesitation; it is reading the room.
- **Avoid repeating someone else's exact line.** Variation keeps the wave interesting.
- **Skip the questions during the peak.** Save them for the release phase.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Mirror the length and tone of the room | Post essays mid-ritual |
| Use the cue exactly as it is presented | Invent your own version of the cue |
