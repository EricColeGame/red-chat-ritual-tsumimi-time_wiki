## What Red Chat Ritual – TSUMIMI TIME Actually Is

Developed and published by Tohakusha Inc. (with CIRCLE LINE GAMES handling Chinese regions), this is a deduction-driven horror adventure built around a single unsettling hook: Mashiro, your favorite streamer, has gone missing, and the only person still broadcasting is Tsumimi. You never control Tsumimi directly. You sit in chat. You decide what to type, when to stay silent, and where to spend a very limited budget — then you spend the daylight hours digging through search results, forum replies, and fan sites to work out what is actually happening behind the cute overlay.

## The Loop: Investigate by Day, Intervene by Night

The structure is the strongest argument for the game, and it is why the reviews are so polarized. Nights are passive-aggressive social engineering; days are detective work.

| Phase | What you do | What it affects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night (stream) | Post comments, fire Mega Chats, send gifts, or deliberately stay quiet | Tsumimi's mood, what she reveals, which branch you enter |
| Day (investigation) | Search keywords, read post replies, check location details, browse fan sites | Unlocks new dialogue options and deduction links |
| Deduction | Connect collected fragments in the Investigation Files | Determines which of the multiple endings you reach |
| Replay | Hold Ctrl during a stream to fast-forward to the next Mega Chat choice | Lets you test alternate choices without re-watching everything |

Because funds are finite, every Mega Chat is a tradeoff. Spending big to push Tsumimi toward a confession may leave you unable to afford the gift that unlocks a later clue. That tension is the actual game — and it is also why the increased starting funds in the first patch mattered so much to early players.

## Seven Habits That Improve the Experience

Community reports and the official play guide converge on a handful of habits that make the 15 days far less frustrating.

| Habit | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Save at the top of the home screen before entering a stream | Lets you retry a Mega Chat branch without replaying the day |
| Hold Ctrl during replays | Skips straight to the next Mega Chat decision point |
| Read replies to posts, not just the posts | The replies routinely contain the location detail you need |
| Check location details explicitly | Search results alone often omit the confirming clue |
| Review the Investigation Files regularly | Prevents you from carrying an unresolved deduction into a deadline |
| Wear headphones | The audio design carries most of the dread; the store page recommends it |
| Keep a written budget per stream | Prevents a mid-week dry spell where you cannot afford a critical Mega Chat |

If you get stuck, the official FAQ lives in the Guides section of the Steam Community hub. Be warned: the back half contains walkthrough spoilers, so read only as far as you need.
