## The Day/Night Loop: How to Split Investigation and Intervention

Think of each of the 15 days as two separate jobs. Daytime is research: keyword searches, replies to posts, location details, and anything unsettling that surfaces in search results. Nighttime is performance: Tsumimi smiles on camera while rumors churn through the comments, and every message you send nudges the story.

| Phase | Your job | Main resource | Most common mistake |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daytime research | Search keywords, read replies, check locations | Time and attention | Skipping reply chains and location notes |
| File review | Cross-check clues in Investigation Files | None | Relying on memory instead of the files |
| Stream opening | Read the chat mood before typing | None | Posting before you understand the topic |
| Mega Chat window | Spend funds to shift the narrative | Money | Spending early on low-information choices |
| Post-stream | Save and note what changed | None | Overwriting the wrong save slot |

The store description asks three questions that double as a strategy guide: what will you say, when will you stay silent, and how will you use your limited funds? Silence is a legitimate tool. A comment you don't post can't derail a thread, and holding funds for a later Mega Chat usually beats scattering small contributions across the first few nights.

### Where Your Money Should Go

Comments are your baseline tool — they shape the tone of chat and let you probe topics without spending. Mega Chats and gifts are the paid levers, which is why the game keeps emphasizing "limited funds." Community reports suggest that early spending feels generous and late-game spending feels tight, so treat the first two or three days as reconnaissance rather than shopping.

| Intervention | Relative weight | Cost | Best used for |

| Mega Chat | High | Paid | Forcing a topic Tsumimi would otherwise avoid |
| Gift | Medium to high | Paid | Shifting mood or drawing attention to a specific thread |

| Step | Action | Why it matters |

## FAQ

**How long is a Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME playthrough?**
The story spans 15 in-game days, each split between daytime investigation and a nighttime stream. Actual playtime depends heavily on how thoroughly you read replies and cross-check Investigation Files, and on whether you fast-forward through replays.

**Can I save mid-stream?**
The game lets you save and load from the top of the home screen. The safer habit is to save before entering a stream so you can replay a Mega Chat decision without redoing the whole day.

**Does my chat actually change the ending?**
Yes. The developers state that your interventions, investigations, deductions, and choices shape Tsumimi's stream and determine how the story ends, with multiple endings available and 16 achievements to chase.

**Is there a faster way to replay for other endings?**
Hold the Ctrl key during a stream to fast-forward to the next Mega Chat choice. Combined with a save parked before the stream, it turns a second Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME playthrough into a short, targeted experiment instead of a full re-run.

The takeaway is straightforward: treat Red Chat Ritual – TSUMIMI TIME as a deduction game wearing a horror mask. Research patiently, spend deliberately, save constantly, and let the fast-forward key do the boring work on replays. Do that, and the fifteen days go from frustrating to genuinely gripping.
