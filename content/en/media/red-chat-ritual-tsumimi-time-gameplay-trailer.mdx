## Comments, Mega Chats, and Gifts: Reading the Chat Mechanics

Three tools sit at the center of every broadcast, and they share one budget. That constraint is the whole design. According to the store description, you intervene through comments, Mega Chats, and gifts, and the game explicitly asks how you will spend limited funds — and when you will choose to say nothing at all.

| Tool | Cost | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Comment | Free | Steering conversation and testing reactions |
| Mega Chat | Paid from your funds | Getting noticed when a normal message gets buried |
| Gift | Paid from your funds | Standing out in the room and shaping the mood |
| Silence | Free, but costly in other ways | The store page treats restraint as a real choice, not a pass |

That last row is the one most trailers skip. Watching the clip, it is easy to assume the game rewards maximum engagement. The framing suggests the opposite: every message is a commitment, and money spent to be seen is money you cannot spend later in the 15-day window.

The Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME gameplay trailer also hints at the deduction layer without explaining it. The store page fills that gap: clues collected during the day are stored in Investigation Files, replies to posts and location details matter, and your conclusions determine how Tsumimi's stream unfolds. Multiple endings are confirmed, and the achievement list — which includes entries named for specific endings alongside one for being a long-time stream viewer — backs that up.

## How to Judge the Trailer Before You Buy

- **Play the demo first.** It exists, it costs nothing, and it will tell you in minutes whether the chat-reading loop clicks for you.
