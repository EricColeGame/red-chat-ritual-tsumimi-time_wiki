## Reading the Chat Cues Correctly

The chat interface is the whole game, which means the signals you need are buried in tone, pacing, and repetition rather than in a menu. Community reports describe three cues worth tracking closely.

- **Response windows.** When the pause before a reply grows noticeably longer, that is usually a prompt waiting on you rather than the game loading.
- **Repetition.** A repeated line is often a nudge, not a glitch. Answering it the same way twice is a common reason players stall.
- **Tonal shift.** A sudden change in the chat's register usually marks a route branch. Match the new tone instead of continuing your previous register.

A useful habit: after each exchange, ask yourself whether the ritual acknowledged your answer. If two replies in a row produce no acknowledgment, you are probably off the intended path and should consider restarting rather than pushing forward.
