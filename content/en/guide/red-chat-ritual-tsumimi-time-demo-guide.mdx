## The Core Loop: Intervention, Investigation, Deduction

Every night, Tsumimi streams. Every night, you get a chance to push her. The game's three pillars are deliberately tangled — what you send changes what she shows you, and what she shows you changes what you can deduce.

| Pillar | What you do | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Intervention | Send Mega Chats and comments during night streams | Her reactions, the stream's mood, which information surfaces |
| Investigation | Study irregularities on camera and fragments off it | Which clues enter your investigation file |
| Deduction | Link fragments and confirm hypotheses | The branch you land on and the ending you reach |

The clever part is the delay. A gift sent late at night doesn't pay off immediately — it comes back as an unexpected reaction during the *next* day's stream. That single design decision is why this Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME demo guide keeps insisting you think one session ahead instead of one message ahead.

### Why the day cycle matters as much as the night

Night is performance; day is processing. During the day you're reviewing what you saw, organizing the investigation file, and deciding which hypothesis is worth confirming. Players who treat daytime as filler tend to arrive at the next stream without a plan, which is exactly when they overspend on Mega Chats out of panic.

## Reading a Tsumimi Stream: Signals Worth Tracking

A Tsumimi broadcast is a wall of noise by design. Chat scrolls, the overlay flickers, she talks over herself. Your job is to filter. The table below is a general tracking framework based on the game's stated design — not a datamined spoiler list — so use it as a checklist rather than gospel.

| Signal | Where it shows up | Why it's worth noting |
|---|---|---|
| Audio drift | Background hum, sudden dead air | Often precedes a tonal shift in the stream |
| Set dressing | Shelves, windows, doorways, cables | Carries fragments of the abandoned village's history |
| Chat velocity | Speed and tone of comments | Reflects how hard you've pushed her that night |
| Callback gifts | The following day's broadcast | Confirms a delayed reaction fired correctly |
| Timestamp gaps | The stream clock or archive | Suggests footage that isn't being shown to you |

Three habits that pay off immediately:

- **Watch the room, not the face.** Background details change between streams more than Tsumimi's expression does.
- **Note anything that repeats.** A phrase, a sound, a camera angle — repetition is the game telling you it's a fragment.
- **Write down what you sent and when.** Because reactions are delayed, your own message log becomes a diagnostic tool.

If you're using a Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME demo guide to prepare for the full release, this is the section to internalize. The story is short — around three hours a run — but it's dense enough that unobserved details are the difference between a clean deduction and a guess.

## Spending Mega Chats and Gifts Without Wasting Them

Mega Chats are paid donation messages, which means the game has given you a budget and a conscience and asked you to negotiate between them. In practice, every message is a trade: information, mood, or chaos.

| Priority | Situation | Why it's worth the spend |
|---|---|---|
| Information | She's about to skip past a detail | A well-timed prompt can pull it back on camera |
| Mood control | She's spiraling and losing the thread | Keeps the stream running long enough to observe |
| Gift timing | Late in a night stream | Sets up a delayed payoff in the next day's broadcast |
| Experiment | You're deliberately testing a branch | The fastest way to see how far the story flexes |

A few practical rules:

- **Ask before you spend.** Comments are free; Mega Chats are not. Confirm the stream is actually stalling before you open the wallet.
- **Don't stack messages in one beat.** One pointed line lands harder than five scattershot ones, and stacking muddies your own record of cause and effect.
- **Save gifts for the dead of night.** The official description specifically ties late-night gifts to next-day reactions, so timing beats volume.
- **Track your pushes.** If the mood curdles, you need to know which message did it.
