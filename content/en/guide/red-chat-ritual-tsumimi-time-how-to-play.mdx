## The Day/Night Loop: Investigate by Day, Intervene by Night

The entire game runs on a repeating two-phase cycle. Understanding that rhythm is the single biggest shortcut to playing well, because most new players either over-spend at night or waste daylight on dead ends.

| Phase | What You Do | What It Costs | Best Practice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day (investigation) | Read posts, check replies, search keywords, dig through fan sites and location details | Time (each day is limited) | Follow every thread with a reply chain before moving on |
| Night (stream) | Watch Tsumimi live, post comments, send Mega Chats, gift items | Real in-game funds | Save before the stream starts, then commit to one strategy |
| Between days | Review the Investigation Files | Nothing | Re-read new entries and connect them to the night's questions |

Night streams are where the horror lives, but daylight is where you earn the right to act. Clues you gather during the day unlock better comment options at night; without them, you're guessing in a chat full of strangers.

### Why silence is a real option

The game explicitly asks three questions: what will you say, when will you stay silent, and how will you spend limited money? Posting a comment isn't always the right move. Some of the most useful moments in a playthrough come from staying quiet and letting a scene play out, or from saving funds for a later day when a single Mega Chat can redirect the entire conversation.

## How to Play Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME Without Wasting Money

Funds are the scarcest resource in the game, and unlike a typical RPG, there is no grinding loop to refill them. You have what you have, spread across 15 days. A patch shortly after launch increased starting funds and added fast-forwarding, which makes early replays far less painful — but budgeting still matters.

| Action | Cost | Story Impact | When to Use It |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard comment | Free | Low to moderate — sets tone, triggers replies | Constantly, to probe reactions and unlock dialogue branches |
| Mega Chat | Expensive | High — can change Tsumimi's behavior mid-stream | Only when you have a specific deduction to test |
| Gift | Moderate to expensive | Shifts mood and trust over time | Early days, to build rapport you can lean on later |
| Silence | Free | Variable — sometimes protective, sometimes costly | When you don't yet understand what you're looking at |

A practical rule of thumb from player experience: treat the first three or four days as reconnaissance. Spend lightly, gather keywords, and bank funds. The mid-game is where money starts buying real information, and the final days punish anyone who burned their budget on cosmetic reactions.

### Track your keywords, not your feelings

The investigation system is keyword-driven. You search a term, get results, and use what you learn to fuel deductions. Keep a mental (or literal) list of the terms the game surfaces — names, locations, usernames, dates — and re-search them as new days unlock new results. A keyword that returns nothing on Day 4 often returns something crucial on Day 9.

## Investigation Files and Deduction Tips

The Investigation Files are your case board. Every clue you collect lands there, and the game expects you to cross-reference rather than memorize. If you're ever stuck in Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME, the answer is almost always an unread reply or an unchecked location detail rather than a hidden mechanic.

| Clue Source | Where to Look | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post replies | Expand every comment thread | Replies often contain corrections, denials, or accidental admissions |
| Location details | Click through attached places | Confirms timelines and contradicts alibis |
| Search results | Re-run old keywords each day | Results change as the story progresses |
| Fan sites | Read the obsessive posts | Community lore in-game hides the most unsettling records |
| Stream chat | Watch how regulars react | Long-time viewers notice things the protagonist misses |

Three habits separate smooth playthroughs from frustrated ones:

- **Expand everything.** A collapsed reply is a hidden clue. The game tells you outright to check replies and location details.
- **Save before every stream.** The home screen supports save and load, and a pre-stream save lets you test a bold Mega Chat and rewind if it backfires.
- **Revisit the Files after each night.** New stream events add context to old clues, and the connection is usually obvious once you look.

## Endings, Achievements, and Replay Strategy

Multiple endings are the point of the whole design. Your interventions, your investigations, your deductions, and your choices all feed into how Tsumimi's stream resolves. Achievement names visible on the store page include entries like "End 6," "End 7," and "End 8," which tells you the ending list is numbered and longer than a simple good/bad split.

| Replay Goal | Strategy | Time Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See a new ending fast | Load a pre-stream save, hold Ctrl to skip dialogue, change one key Mega Chat | Very low |
| Unlock ending achievements | Work backward from the ending numbers you're missing | Moderate |
| Full clue completion | Fresh run, expand every reply, re-search all keywords daily | High |
| Low-spend challenge | Comments only, gifts reserved for the final act | High risk, high insight |

Because saves are available from the home screen, you never need to replay the whole 15 days to experiment. The efficient pattern is: save at the start of a day, save again before the stream, then branch. If you're chasing a specific ending in Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME, changing a single Mega Chat decision is often enough — the game rewards precision over volume.

One last tip that saves real time: don't skip the quiet scenes. The horror in this game is atmospheric rather than jump-scare driven, and the small conversational beats during streams are frequently where a deduction clicks into place.

## FAQ

**Is Red Chat Ritual TSUMIMI TIME hard to learn how to play?**
No. The mechanics are simple — comment, spend, investigate, deduce. The difficulty comes from resource pressure and knowing when to stay silent, not from complicated controls.

**How long is a single playthrough?**
The story spans 15 in-game days. Because save and load are available from the home screen, you can branch from any day instead of restarting the whole campaign.

**Can I skip scenes when replaying for a different ending?**
Yes. Hold the Ctrl key during a stream to fast-forward to the next Mega Chat choice, which makes ending hunting far quicker.

**Is there a demo and official help if I get stuck?**
A free demo is available on Steam, and the developers published an official Hints & FAQ in the Steam Community guides plus a play guide in Steam News covering saving, time management, and keyword investigation.
