## The Core Loop: Investigate by Day, Intervene by Night

The pitch is compact and slightly cruel: Mashiro, your favorite streamer, is missing. Tsumimi — cheerful, camera-ready, apparently fine — is still streaming. You are a viewer with a wallet, a keyboard, and a limited number of days to work out what connects the two.

| Phase | What you do | What it costs you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day | Search keywords, read posts and replies, check locations, review your Investigation Files | Time |
| Night | Watch Tsumimi's stream; choose comments, Mega Chats, and gifts — or stay silent | Money and attention |
| Between | Connect fragments, form deductions, commit to an interpretation | Irreversible story branches |

Two resources govern everything. Time decides how much you can investigate. Money decides how loudly you can speak in chat. Blow your budget early on a dramatic Mega Chat and you may find yourself silent on the night it actually mattered. That tension is the whole design, and it is why the English localization is not a cosmetic detail — the deduction systems lean on reading replies, cross-referencing location details, and noticing which search results feel wrong.

### Why silence is a real choice

The game explicitly asks: what will you say, when will you stay silent, and how will you spend limited funds? Community reports from early players suggest that aggressive intervention is not automatically the "good" path. Sometimes watching, logging, and saving your money produces cleaner information than pushing Tsumimi toward a reaction. Treat your comment box as a resource, not a reflex.

## Practical Tips for Your First 15 Days

These are general, spoiler-free habits that hold up regardless of which route you take.

- **Save before every stream.** You can save and load from the top of the home screen, and a pre-stream save makes it painless to test a different Mega Chat choice.
- **Read the replies, not just the posts.** The game's own guidance points at reply chains and location details as core deduction material. Most missed clues live one layer deeper than the surface post.
- **Lean on the Investigation Files.** Collected information is reviewable in one place, so re-read it before committing to a theory instead of trusting memory.
- **Hold Ctrl to fast-forward on replays.** During a stream, holding Ctrl skips to the next Mega Chat decision point — invaluable when you are chasing a second or third ending.
- **Check the official guides before you rage-quit a bad ending.** The Steam News "TSUMIMI TIME Play Guide" covers saves, time, and money management, and the official FAQ in the Steam Community Guides section covers progression. Be warned: the back half of that FAQ contains walkthrough spoilers.

That combination is worth taking seriously — this is a game about obsession with a streamer, and it does not handle the subject lightly.
