A few habits will save you a replay:

- **Save before entering a stream.** The save/load option sits at the top of the home screen, and a save point before each broadcast lets you test different chat choices without redoing investigation work.

- **Play with headphones.** The store page recommends headphones or earbuds, and for a game where the tension comes from a chat window and a smiling streamer, audio does a lot of the work.
- **Budget your funds early.** Gifts and Mega Chats are limited, and spending freely in the first few days can leave you silent when a critical moment arrives.
