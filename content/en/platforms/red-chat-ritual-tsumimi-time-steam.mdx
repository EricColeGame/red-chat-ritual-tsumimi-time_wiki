## The Day-and-Night Loop: Investigate, Then Intervene

This is the engine that drives everything, and understanding it early saves you a lot of wasted in-game currency.

**By day,** you research. Search keywords, read replies to posts, and check location details — the store page explicitly warns that replies and locations matter, not just headlines. Everything you gather lands in your Investigation Files, which you can reopen at any time to re-read clues before committing to a theory.

**By night,** you watch Tsumimi's stream and act. You have three levers: comments, Mega Chats, and gifts. Each one costs you something — attention, money, or a piece of your credibility with the chat — and you are working from a limited budget across the whole 15-day run.

| Phase | Your role | Main tools | What it affects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daytime investigation | Researcher | Search terms, post replies, location details, fan sites | Clue pool in Investigation Files |
| Nighttime stream | Viewer / intervener | Comments, Mega Chats, gifts | Tsumimi's behavior and story branches |
| Deduction phase | Detective | Connecting fragments in the Investigation Files | Which ending you unlock |
| Replay | Speedrunner | Ctrl to fast-forward to the next Mega Chat choice | Testing alternate branches quickly |

Silence is a real option too. The game asks bluntly what you will say, when you will stay quiet, and how you will spend limited funds — three separate decisions that each push the narrative. Community reports suggest the endings are gated behind combinations of these choices rather than a single "correct" answer, and the achievement names (End 6, End 7, End 8, Long-time stream viewer) hint at how many branches exist.

### Practical advice for the loop

- Save at the home screen before entering a stream, so you can reload and test a different Mega Chat response without replaying the whole day.
- Hold Ctrl during a stream to fast-forward to the next Mega Chat choice — essential on repeat runs.
- Read replies and location text even when a post looks like filler; the game flags this specifically.
- Play with headphones. The audio design is called out on the store page for a reason.

## Tips for Your First 15 Days

A short list that will save you from restarting on day twelve:

1. **Front-load your investigation.** Clues you skip early are harder to interpret later when the story accelerates.
2. **Budget your Mega Chats.** Treat your funds like a finite resource across all 15 days, not a per-night allowance.
3. **Try silence once.** Choosing not to comment is a valid branch and can reveal different reactions from Tsumimi.
4. **Use the official FAQ carefully.** It lives in the Steam Community Guides section, and the second half contains walkthrough spoilers.
5. **Expect horror, not just mystery.** The mature content description flags psychological horror, violence and blood, and references to self-harm and suicide. There is no explicit sexual content, but the themes are heavy.
6. **Replay with Ctrl held down.** A second run takes a fraction of the time once you can skip to the next decision point.
